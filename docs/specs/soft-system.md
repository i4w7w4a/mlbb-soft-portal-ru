# SOFT System Spec

## Product Role

SOFT is the primary brand object, not a regular feature filter. It changes:

- content ranking and prominence
- the look and feel of major surfaces
- badge language and CTA hierarchy
- section ordering on the homepage and hero pages

## UI Rules

- SOFT is always visible in the floating header.
- SOFT uses a distinct capsule form, glow ring, and pulse treatment.
- When SOFT mode is active, interface accents shift toward cyan-gold highlights, surfaces gain stronger aura treatment, and SOFT content jumps ahead in ranked collections.
- SOFT articles always carry a premium badge and priority card variant.

## Content Rules

- `isSoft` marks an article as part of the SOFT editorial layer.
- `isSoftFeatured` marks heroes that deserve stronger SOFT prominence.
- `/soft` is a curated hub, not a generic filtered list.
- Hero pages only render the SOFT rail when hero-linked SOFT stories exist.
